Chapter 28: Addressable RGB (ARGB)

The chip integrates a set of ARGB (Addressable RGB) modules, supporting configurable high/low level timing and period for 0-code and 1-code output, and supporting DMA.

The ARGB module can implement single-data-line RGB light strip timing control, supporting single-wire RGB daisy-chaining. Compared with traditional RGB, it can support driving more different models of ARGB lamp control ICs, producing more lighting effects.

28.1 Main Features

  • Supports configurable high/low level timing and period for 0-code and 1-code output
  • Supports DMA
